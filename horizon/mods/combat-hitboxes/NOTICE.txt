Combat Hitboxes (Horizon port)

Original mod: Combat Hitboxes 1.0.11 by Sootysplash, licensed under the
Apache License 2.0 (LICENSE.txt). The icon is the original mod's.

This port re-implements the mod's behaviour against the Horizon mod API. Changes:
- Rewritten in Rust as a Horizon .hmod; the settings use Horizon's settings page
  instead of Cloth Config.
- The "hide stuck arrows" option is left out: Horizon draws no arrows stuck in
  players.
- Colours are picked from a fixed palette instead of a colour picker.
