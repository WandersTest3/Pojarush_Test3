3D Skin Layers (Test Port) for Horizon

This is an unofficial, independent reimplementation of the idea of the
Minecraft mod "3D Skin Layers", made only to test the Horizon mod API in the
Pojarush_Test3 launcher. It contains no code, textures or other files from the
original mod.

Original mod: 3D Skin Layers by tr7zw
